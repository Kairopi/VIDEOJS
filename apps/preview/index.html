Tint Tint

A Video.js player that wears the film's colours.

Choose a film

Each card is the film's own fingerprint.

    One image becomes the whole interface.

    Tint reads the storyboard Mux already makes for timeline previews. Nothing samples the playing video, so it costs nothing per frame.

    Scene colours, used for the accent as you watch

      Made to work for everyone.

      Keyboard
      Every control is reachable. Press [ and ] to jump between scenes.
      Focus you can see
      One ring everywhere: the scene colour with a dark halo, clear over any frame.
      Readable colour
      Each scene colour is set to a lightness that reads on the dark controls.
      Calm on request
      With reduced motion, the fold and the colour easing switch off.
      Thumb-sized
      44 px controls, bigger in fullscreen. Hover effects only where there's hover.
      Any size
      Controls reflow at four widths, from a phone to a cinema screen.

      Use it on your site.

      Copy the skin's src folder into your project, then wrap any video that has a thumbnails track.

      <video-player content-title="Sprite Fright">
  <!-- the markup from src/video/skin.html -->
  <mux-video
    src="https://stream.mux.com/PLAYBACK_ID.m3u8"
    playsinline crossorigin="anonymous"></mux-video>
</video-player>

<script type="module">
  import '@videojs/html/video/player';
  import '@videojs/html/media/mux-video';
  import './src/video/skin.ts';
</script>
