Tint

A Video.js player that wears the film's colours.

Four films. One spectrum.

Every frame, sorted by colour. Point at a colour to watch that moment.

One image becomes the whole interface.

Tint reads the storyboard that Mux already makes for timeline previews. It never samples the playing video, so it costs nothing per frame.

Made to work for everyone.

Every claim here can be checked with Test the player, under the player.

Keyboard
Every control is reachable, menus close with Escape, and [ and ] jump between scenes.
Focus you can see
One ring everywhere: the scene colour with a dark halo, clear over any frame.
Readable colour
Scene colours are set to a lightness that reads on the controls. Text on the accent picks black or white.
Calm on request
With reduced motion, the fold, the colour easing and the loading sweep switch off.
Thumb-sized
44 px controls, bigger in fullscreen. Hover effects only where there's hover.
Any size, any language
Controls reflow at four widths and mirror for right-to-left languages.

Use it on your site.

Copy the skin's src folder into your project, then wrap any video that has a thumbnails track.

<video-player content-title="Sprite Fright">
  <!-- the markup from src/video/skin.html -->
  <mux-video
    src="https://stream.mux.com/PLAYBACK_ID.m3u8"
    playsinline crossorigin="anonymous"></mux-video>
</video-player>

<script type="module">
  import '@videojs/html/video/player';
  import '@videojs/html/media/mux-video';
  import './src/video/skin.ts';
</script>
